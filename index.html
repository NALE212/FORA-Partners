Questions

contact@forapartners.eu

Who do you work with?

Suppliers who want to sell to retailers, and retailers looking for new suppliers. We are not limited to one industry or country.

How do you find the right retailers?

We start from your products, prices and capacity and look for retailers whose range fits. We only introduce you when there is real interest.

How long does it take?

Most suppliers meet their first retailers within a few weeks, depending on the product and market.

What does it cost?

It depends on the scope. After a short call you get a clear offer, with no obligation.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. We only share your details with partners you approve, and we sign an NDA on request.

I'm a retailer. Can you help me too?

Yes. Tell us what you want to stock and we look for suppliers that fit.
